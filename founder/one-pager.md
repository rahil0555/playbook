# Edurance: one page

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed): year 1 operating loss on the parent-pays model. The fix to test is the school-paid licence. Details in `business-plan.md`.

**The problem.**
- In mixed-ability classes, students fall behind on specific topics and never catch up.
- Teachers can't diagnose 40 students one by one.
- Parents pay for tuition without knowing which chapters are actually weak.

**The customer.**
- **Schools** in Hyderabad and Secunderabad choose it: 150 private schools mapped across 4 zones.
- **Parents** of Class 4–10 students in those schools pay to continue it.

**The offer: Gap-to-Grade.**
- A free in-class diagnostic and a gap report for every student.
- 20 minutes a day of practice set by the class teacher on the child's own gap topics, mapped to the school's syllabus.
- AI explanations in text and voice, a weekly parent view, and a parent-set time limit.
- An end-of-term re-test. If the gap topics show no improvement, the next term is free.
- A data promise co-signed by the school.

**Price:**
- **₹299/month** or **₹999/term**, plus ₹149/month for each extra child.
- Founding Families pay ₹249/month at the first 3 schools.
- The school keeps 30%.
- Being tested alongside: a **school-paid licence at ₹1,200 per student per year**.

**The evidence so far:**
- **Board:** 3 × "fund if", average 4.3/10. A real product, a reachable market, no evidence yet of adoption, use, learning or payment.
- **Simulated parent panel** (20 parents): 30% → 45% buy after the offer rework. Treat it as an upper bound.
- **Competitors:** not yet mapped (`/founder-competitors`). Public prices suggest ₹299/month sits above PhysicsWallah batch pricing per month, well below Extramarks list prices, and below tuition.
- **Real tests:** none yet.

**What the money is for.**
- About **₹1.54 lakh** over year 1, out of an under-₹2 lakh budget, with founders unpaid.
- Pilot AI cost: ₹30k. Legal review (consent and data promise): ₹25k. Pilot materials: ₹15k. Contingency: ₹15k.
- Running costs of ~₹16k/month: hosting, travel and printing.
