# Board brief: Edurance

(Written for board members who have not seen the founder conversation. Everything below is what the founders stated. Items marked UNKNOWN have not been provided and must not be invented.)

## The idea
Edurance is an AI-powered learning platform for school students. It uses diagnostic assessments and learning data to understand each student's level, strengths, weaknesses, pace and knowledge gaps, then delivers adaptive learning, AI-generated explanations, voice-based learning (text-to-speech narration), personalized learning paths, progress and mastery tracking, and parent- and school-facing visibility. Long-term vision: an AI tutor that continuously adapts to each student, making personalized learning accessible and scalable.

## Customer
- Primary users: school students.
- Key customer and distribution channel: schools.
- Stakeholders and possible payers: parents.
- Initial approach: partner with schools to bring Edurance to groups of students, not individual student acquisition.

## Market
Hyderabad and Secunderabad, Telangana, India. A prospect universe of 150 schools across four geographic zones, each managed by a founder. Expansion depends on validation and resources.

## Price
₹299 per paid user under the current subscription model. UNKNOWN: billing period (monthly/yearly/term), who pays (school or parent), school revenue share, willingness to pay, conversion rate, net revenue per user.

## Stage
Working web-first product built on Flutter, Supabase, OpenAI APIs and Vercel. Built: authentication, diagnostic testing and persistence, lesson resumption, adaptive teaching flows, AI explanations, TTS narration, audio replay and caching, parent-facing functionality. A separate early-years module (PlayLab: alphabet, phonics, word building) exists but is not yet merged.
UNKNOWN: signed schools, pilots, active users, engagement, retention, learning-outcome evidence, operating costs (including AI API cost per student), conversion rates. The October plan's funnel targets are planning assumptions, not results.

## Go-to-market
School-led B2B2C: school outreach → decision-maker meetings and demos → proposals → signed pilots → educator orientation → student onboarding → baseline diagnostics → learning activation → progress review → paid conversion and expansion.

## Team
Four co-founders covering technology, business and partnerships, operations, and product. Exact allocation of roles and accountability not yet confirmed.

## Budget
Not specified. Prefer low-cost validation; do not assume spending capacity.

## The founders' own open questions
- Will schools adopt and commit to paid or structured pilots?
- Will students use it consistently after onboarding?
- Will parents and schools see enough value to justify ₹299 per paid user?
- Does it improve learning outcomes or close learning gaps?
- What share of prospects become signed pilots and paying users?
- Can the team deliver onboarding, support and reliability as adoption grows?
- Can it acquire and retain users economically after school revenue share and operating costs?
