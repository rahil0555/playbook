## What would flip a no

- If the re-test shows his weak topics really improved and the school gives me a copy of the signed data agreement, I would pay the ₹249 rate.
- The end-of-term re-test and my child's actual school exam marks both improving in the gap topics, confirmed by the class teacher at the parent-teacher meeting.
- The end-of-term re-test showing clear improvement on his gap topics in the school's own chapters, plus the teacher telling me it actually helped in class.
- If the end-of-term re-test showed his gap topics clearly closed and his tuition teacher could also see the report and use it, so it replaces some tuition cost instead of adding to it.
- If the end-of-term re-test clearly shows his weak chapters improved and the tuition sir himself says it helps, I might pay so I can cut down tuition hours.
- The class teacher telling us on the WhatsApp group that she has reviewed the content against the syllabus, and the re-test actually showing my child's gap topics improved.
- If the end-of-term re-test clearly shows my child's weak topics improved and the school makes it part of regular homework, I might pay the ₹999 term price for one child.
- If the end-of-term re-test shows clear improvement and the teacher tells me at the parent-teacher meeting that it helped, and the school keeps assigning it as homework next term.
- If the end-of-term re-test showed clear improvement that my tuition teacher had not managed, and the school folded the cost into the fees or I could drop some tuition hours because of it.
- If the end-of-term re-test showed clear gains our tuition teacher hadn't managed, and the class teacher kept using it as part of the school's own homework, so it wasn't something extra I had to push at home.
- If the end-of-term re-test shows his gap topics really closed and the class teacher tells me it helped more than tuition did, so I could cut down tuition hours instead of paying for both.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
